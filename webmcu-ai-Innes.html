WebMCU AI on device vision Getting Started Link

Goal

  1. Open the WebMCU ai link above on your cell phone.
  2. Rename / set your classes to: Mouse, Pen, and Unknown
  3. use your phone's camera to collect image samples for each of the 3 classes and train the model on your device
  4. test your model live on your phone screen
  5. take screenshot proof of your model working identifying each item

Proof of Completetion (Upload Requirement)
